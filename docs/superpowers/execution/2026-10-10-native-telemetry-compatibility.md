## Logging and monitoring

`BoundedAsyncLogAppender` retains only bounded approved metadata before its queue, excluding message text, formatted arguments, token material and throwable stacks. Its 1,024-entry queue is nonblocking (`neverBlock=true`, discard threshold zero). Nonblocking semaphore credits bound the sum of queued, exporting and producer-reserved original events to 1,024; the single export sink releases credits after processing. Exhausted credits reject the event and increment the exact observed drop count. Producers take no export monitor and never wait for stdout or a queue credit. JSON serialization/stdout run on the export worker. The process log lifecycle stops after native lifecycle phases and permits at most two seconds for export. Residual original credits, including a blocked current export, after the timed drain are exposed as unresolved separately from proven overflow drops; blocked stdout is not described as physically settled or lossless.

Helm `monitoring.enabled` gates installation. `dashboard`, `rules`, and `podMonitor` independently install the Grafana dashboard ConfigMap, correctly shaped PrometheusRule, and private-health PodMonitor. The PodMonitor targets the actual deployment name/instance/cell labels and private health port. These resources require the operator's monitoring CRDs/selection configuration; no chart deployment is executed.
